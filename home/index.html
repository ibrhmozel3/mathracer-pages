Why kids (and parents) like it

🏎️

Endless racing

Every correct answer pushes the car further. One wrong answer breaks the streak — so speed and accuracy both matter.

🔥

Streak multiplier

Chain correct answers to multiply your score. Smart wrong-answer choices keep every round genuinely challenging.

🎨

Garage & customization

Earn coins by playing, then spend them on new car models and paint colors in the Garage. Nothing costs real money.

🎯

Practice Mode

Pick exactly which times tables to drill, and let a grown-up see which facts a child gets wrong most — hidden behind a simple parent check.

🌍

Three languages

Fully playable in English, Turkish, and Spanish, switchable any time from the menu.

🔒

Kid-safe by design

No accounts, no chat, no ads today, and no data collection of any kind. Everything stays on the device.
